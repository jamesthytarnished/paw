OUR PURPOSE

Every Pet Deserves a Chance at a Better Life.

The PAWS Foundation supports animal shelters, rescue organizations, and communities working to protect animals in need. Through donations, resources, and community partnerships, we help provide food, medical care, shelter, and opportunities for homeless pets to find loving homes.
