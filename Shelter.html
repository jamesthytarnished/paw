Animal Shelters & Rescues

Animal shelters and rescues are lifelines for pets in need. They provide safe places for animals, help them recover, and give them opportunities to find loving homes. Explore our PAWS resources to learn about animal welfare, fostering, volunteering, and supporting local shelters. Discover how you can make a positive difference in the lives of animals in need!
